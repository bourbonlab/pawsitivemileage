# PawsitiveMileage — Product Summary

PawsitiveMileage is a serverless vehicle fuel tracking and maintenance management app. Users log fuel purchases with minimal input (date, amount spent, gallons, odometer) and the system auto-calculates MPG, cost per mile, price per gallon, and miles between fillups.

Key capabilities:
- Multi-vehicle "Garage" with archive support
- Fuel fillup tracking with automatic metric calculation
- Admin-configurable maintenance schemas and reminders
- Analytics dashboards with per-vehicle charts and year-over-year comparisons
- Shareable summary cards and CSV/PDF data export
- Admin panel for user management, schema CRUD, and platform monitoring

Target: individual vehicle owners and multi-vehicle households. Initial scale under 1,000 users.

Domains:
- Web: `pawsitivemileage.bourbonlab.net`
- API: `pawsitivemileageapi.bourbonlab.net`
